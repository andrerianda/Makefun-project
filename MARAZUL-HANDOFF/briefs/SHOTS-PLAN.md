# Judged screenshots plan (proposed to the owner 18 Nov 08:55 UTC)
The judge sees one sheet: picture 1 large (fitted 768x480), pictures 2-4 small beneath; the first criterion counts what the pictures of the game itself show, and a picture that shows several systems at once shows more of the game. All are real GPU renders of the game, 1280x800, under 256 KiB, through the game's own cameras (no low cinematic cuts), HUD on.
1. COVER - golden-hour police chase on Bulevar Dorado: the lead's coupe sliding through the curve, sea, beach, palms and towers in the low sun, 3-4 cruisers with red/blue bars closing, the helicopter's searchlight if it reads; PLUS (owner, 09:00) a burning wreck - a crashed police cruiser against a palm or lamp post at the side of the frame, real fire with a dark smoke column rising against the sunset sky, debris on the road (effects' fire and vehicle damage must read as volumetric flames, not flat sheets, and must not wash the frame orange); HUD: wanted stars, minimap with police blips, speedometer, a mission objective line. Chase camera per the owner's numbers.
2. AIM - on-foot firefight in Barrio Hierro (dense, worn street: dumpsters, bins, graffiti, roll-up doors, wires), over-the-shoulder aim camera above the head, pistol at shoulder height per the owner's correction, an enemy crew across the street, muzzle flash frozen, ammo card, objective line.
3. NIGHT - Gran Via or Centro at night after rain: lit windows, neon signs, wet asphalt reflections, crowd on the pavement, traffic head and tail lamps, the lead walking (close GTA-6-style on-foot camera).
4. SCALE - from the sea or the air at sunset: the skyline, beach, pier and marina, a boat or the news helicopter in frame, towers with lit windows.
Each scene: several takes, pick by looking at the 768x480 sheet and, later, the mock judge (needs the owner's OpenRouter key).

OWNER CHANGE (09:58 UTC): no night picture in the judged set - "terlalu gelap, nanti AI judge-nya ribet" (too dark, the AI judge will struggle). Picture 3 becomes DUSK on foot: Gran Via, Centro or the beach strip at early blue hour (about 18:50-19:15, sun just set, sky still coloured orange-violet, street lamps and neon just on, windows starting to light), the lead walking with the close camera, crowd, traffic with lamps on, wet or dry street. Every judged picture must read clearly at 768x480: mean luminance at least ~85/255 and no large crushed-black areas; the skyline picture at sunset, not after dark.
