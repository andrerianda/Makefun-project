## Your job: motorcycles, boats and helicopters
Model, bake and ship these six vehicles as game-ready .glb assets, with exactly these asset ids: sport_motorcycle, chopper_motorcycle, speedboat, police_boat, police_helicopter, news_helicopter. The motorcycles are ridden by the player through traffic; the boats are driven across the bay and chased by the police boat; the police helicopter hunts the player with a searchlight at high wanted levels and the news helicopter circles the action. They must read as real machines, not toys.
- Motorcycles: a real frame and proportions; engine block with cylinders, cooling fins or radiator, exhaust headers and silencer; front fork with stanchions, mudguard and brake discs with calipers; swingarm, chain or belt, rear shock; spoked or cast wheels with real tyre profiles; tank, seat, handlebars with grips, levers and mirrors, instruments, headlamp, tail lamp, indicators, footpegs, side stand. The sport bike is faired, compact and tucked; the chopper is long and low with a raked fork, a fat rear tyre, high bars and chrome.
- Boats: a real planing hull with a deep vee, chine and spray rails, a deck with non-slip areas, windscreen, helm console with wheel and throttle, seats, bow rail, cleats, a stern platform and outboard engines with cowlings and propellers. The speedboat is a sleek open sports boat about 8.5 m long; the police boat is a rigid patrol boat about 9.5 m with a small wheelhouse or T-top, a light bar, a searchlight and fenders.
- Helicopters: a light twin-seat-row utility helicopter shape with a glazed cockpit, doors, skids, tail boom, fin and tail rotor, engine cowling with intakes and exhaust, rotor mast with hub, pitch links and three or four blades with a slight droop, cabin interior with seats, panel and controls. The police one carries a searchlight under the nose, a camera turret and a light; the news one carries a camera ball under the nose and antenna pods.

You own: art/blender/assets/motorcycles/**, art/blender/assets/boats/**, art/blender/assets/helicopters/** (asset scripts and your underscore-prefixed shared helpers in each), game/assets/motorcycles/**, game/assets/boats/**, game/assets/helicopters/**, and a new registry module game/src/assets/registry/motorcyclesBoatsHelicopters.js (add its one line to registry/index.js as the rules describe). Scratch: /tmp/marazul/craft/. Other artists are building cars and trucks in other directories under art/blender/assets/; do not depend on them.

Order: sport_motorcycle first, proven in Cycles and in the engine viewer, then the chopper, the speedboat, the police boat, then the helicopters.

## Contract with the game (fixed; other agents code against it)
- Real dimensions: sport_motorcycle about 2.05 m long with a 1.42 m wheelbase and 0.82 m seat height; chopper_motorcycle about 2.45 m long with a 1.75 m wheelbase and 0.68 m seat height; speedboat about 8.5 x 2.5 m; police_boat about 9.5 x 3.0 m; helicopters about 10.5 m fuselage and tail, 2.9 m high, 10.5 m main rotor. If game/src/catalog/vehicleCatalog.js exists when you start a vehicle, take dimensions, seats and typical colours from there.
- Origins and facing follow the conventions: motorcycles on the ground midway between the axles; boats at the waterline (the plane the hull floats on at rest is Z = 0 in Blender) at mid length; helicopters on the ground under the rotor mast, resting on their skids.
- Motorcycle nodes: wheel_f and wheel_r (origin at the hub, spinning about local X); fork (the whole steered assembly: front wheel's fork legs, mudguard, handlebars, headlamp and instruments, with its origin on the steering axis and its local Z in Blender along that axis; wheel_f is a child of it); swingarm (origin at its pivot; wheel_r is a child of it). Anchors: anchor_seat_driver and anchor_seat_passenger_1 (the seated person's hip joints, pointing forward), anchor_grip_l and anchor_grip_r (centres of the handlebar grips, children of the fork), anchor_foot_l and anchor_foot_r (the footpegs), anchor_stand (where the rider stands to mount, on the ground at the left, facing the bike), anchor_light_head_l (one headlamp), anchor_light_tail_l, anchor_exhaust_1, anchor_camera, anchor_plate_r. One collider: col_box_body.
- Boat nodes: steering_wheel (origin at its hub, local Z in Blender along the column toward the helmsman), engine_l and engine_r (or engine for a single outboard; origin on the steering pivot at the transom, the game swings them to steer), propeller_l and propeller_r (children of the engines, spinning about their shaft axis, which is their local Y in Blender). Anchors: anchor_seat_driver, anchor_seat_passenger_1..3, anchor_board (on deck where a person steps aboard), anchor_wake (at the waterline at the stern centre), anchor_spray_l and anchor_spray_r (at the chines forward), anchor_light_head_l (the bow or mast light), anchor_camera; anchor_siren and anchor_searchlight on the police boat. Colliders: col_hull_hull (a convex hull of the hull) and col_box_cabin if there is a wheelhouse.
- Helicopter nodes: rotor_main (origin at the hub, spinning about the vertical axis, which is local Z in Blender), rotor_tail (origin at its hub, spinning about local X), door_fl and door_fr (origin on the hinge), searchlight on the police helicopter (origin at its gimbal; the game aims it). Also ship, as part of each rotor node, a second child mesh named rotor_main_blur and rotor_tail_blur: a thin translucent disc with a soft radial streak texture in a cutout or glass role, which the game shows instead of the blades at speed. Anchors: anchor_seat_driver, anchor_seat_passenger_1..3, anchor_door_driver (on the ground where a person stands to board), anchor_searchlight (police), anchor_camera, anchor_light_head_l (the landing light), anchor_light_tail_l (the tail beacon). Colliders: col_box_cabin, col_box_tail, col_cyl_rotor (a flat cylinder for the main rotor disc).
- Materials by role: paint for the main colour (recoloured by the game, with clear coat); paint_secondary for a second livery colour; glass for screens, canopies and transparent lenses; lamp_head, lamp_tail, lamp_brake and lamp_indicator_l/r on the motorcycles; lamp_head, lamp_nav_red, lamp_nav_green and lamp_tail on boats and helicopters; lamp_siren_a and lamp_siren_b on the police boat and police helicopter; lamp_searchlight where there is one. Anchors anchor_decal_side_l and anchor_decal_side_r mark the flattest side area for painted lettering (police markings, the news station's name), with the usable width and height as extras. Everything else bakes into the atlas with ambient occlusion.
- Levels of detail: motorcycles lod0 about 9,000 to 12,000 triangles, lod1 3,000, lod2 700; boats lod0 about 10,000 to 14,000, lod1 3,500, lod2 900; helicopters lod0 about 12,000 to 16,000, lod1 4,000, lod2 1,000.
- Size: at most about 430 kB per vehicle and 2.6 MiB for all six; set ASSET['budget'] accordingly.

In the registry, document for each vehicle its nodes, anchors and materials and the numbers the physics needs (wheel radii and wheelbase, hull length, beam and draught, rotor radius and skid height). Repeat those numbers as a table in your report.
