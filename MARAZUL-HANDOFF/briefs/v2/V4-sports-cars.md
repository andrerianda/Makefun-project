## Your job: the hero cars
Model, bake and ship these four cars as game-ready .glb assets, with exactly these asset ids: sports_coupe, supercar, muscle_car, convertible_cruiser. These are the cars the player steals and drives, so one of them sits in the middle of the cover screenshot, seen from a chase camera three to six metres behind and slightly above, in low golden sunlight. They are the most looked-at models in the game and must be the best: desirable, original designs with correct stance and proportions, flowing body surfaces with crisp character lines and believable reflections in the clear-coated paint, wide wheel arches with depth, detailed alloy wheels with low-profile tyres, brake discs and calipers, sculpted headlamp and tail-lamp units with lenses and inner detail, grilles and intakes with depth, splitters and diffusers where they fit, mirrors, door handles, wipers, exhaust tips, panel gaps, and an interior that holds up through the glass and, on the convertible, in the open: seats with bolsters and stitching lines, dashboard, instrument cowl, steering wheel, centre console, door cards. The rear three-quarter view matters most. No toy shapes.

You own: art/blender/assets/sports_cars/** (asset scripts and your underscore-prefixed shared helpers), game/assets/sports_cars/**, a new registry module game/src/assets/registry/sportsCars.js (add its one line to registry/index.js as the rules describe). Scratch: /tmp/marazul/sports-cars/. Another artist is building the everyday cars in art/blender/assets/vehicles/ at the same time; do not depend on that directory.

Work in this order. First write your shared helpers (a body builder that lofts a car body from side, top and section profiles so each car is parameters and curves rather than a new program; wheels and tyres; lamps; glass; mirrors; interior; underbody; the standard nodes, anchors and colliders), prove them on sports_coupe until it looks right in Cycles and in the engine viewer, then make the others, each with its own character: the supercar low, wide and mid-engined; the muscle car long-hooded and upright with a wide rear; the convertible cruiser a long, relaxed open-top boulevard car with the roof down and a tonneau behind the seats.

## Contract with the game (fixed; other agents code against it)
- Dimensions are real: for example sports_coupe about 4.5 x 1.87 x 1.30 m, supercar 4.6 x 2.0 x 1.15, muscle_car 4.9 x 1.93 x 1.38, convertible_cruiser 5.1 x 1.9 x 1.35. If game/src/catalog/vehicleCatalog.js exists when you start a car, take its dimensions, seats and typical colours from there.
- Origin on the ground at the centre of the wheelbase, facing forward (see the conventions).
- Separate nodes: wheel_fl, wheel_fr, wheel_rl, wheel_rr (origin at the hub, spinning about local X; the front ones are steered by the game about the vertical axis through the hub, so give them clearance); door_fl and door_fr (origin on the hinge line at the front edge, glass and door card move with them); hood and trunk (origin on the hinge; on the mid-engined car the rear lid is trunk); bumper_f and bumper_r (the game detaches them in crashes); steering_wheel (origin at its hub, its local Z in Blender pointing along the column toward the driver, the game turns it about that axis); window_front, window_rear, window_l, window_r (the glass panes not in doors, so the game can shatter them; leave out what a car does not have). Everything else is the body.
- Anchors: anchor_seat_driver and anchor_seat_passenger_1 (plus _2 and _3 where there is a rear bench), marking where the seated person's hip joints are and pointing the way they face; anchor_door_driver and anchor_door_passenger (on the ground where a person stands to get in, facing the door); anchor_light_head_l/r and anchor_light_tail_l/r; anchor_exhaust_1 and anchor_exhaust_2; anchor_camera (chase camera target, about roof height at the centre); anchor_plate_f and anchor_plate_r (centre of the licence plate recess, facing outward; the game paints plates).
- Materials by role: paint for the body colour (recoloured by the game, with clear coat); paint_secondary for an optional stripe or two-tone area; glass for windows and transparent lamp lenses; lamp_head, lamp_tail, lamp_brake, lamp_reverse, lamp_indicator_l, lamp_indicator_r. Everything else bakes into the atlas (tyres, rims, calipers, grilles, trim, lamp interiors, the interior, the underbody) with ambient occlusion.
- Colliders: one col_box_body for the lower body and one col_box_cabin for the greenhouse; no wheel colliders.
- Levels of detail: lod0 about 16,000 to 20,000 triangles including wheels and interior, lod1 about 5,000, lod2 about 1,000 to 1,200 with no interior and simple wheels. The body mesh of lod0 should be evenly tessellated (no edge longer than about 0.3 m) because the game deforms it in crashes.
- Size: at most about 650 kB per car and 2.6 MiB for all four; set ASSET['budget'] with the reason (hero vehicle). A 2048 atlas is justified here if it shows.

In the registry, document for each car its nodes, anchors, materials and the wheel radius and track the physics needs. In your report give a table of wheel radius, wheelbase, track width and seat count per car, and attach for each car the path of one rear three-quarter engine picture taken with warm low light.
