You are the environment artist for plants and leisure on MARAZUL. Read /tmp/marazul/briefs/ART-RULES.md in full first: it gives the project, the rules you must not break, the pipeline and what to report. Then read art/README.md and art/blender/assets/vegetation/royal_palm_a.py: that palm was written by the pipeline's author as an example of building from baked pieces with hand-made levels of detail, and is now yours to keep, improve or replace.

## Your job: the green and the beach
Palms against a golden sky are the signature of this city and stand in almost every screenshot, so the plants must look alive and real, not like green lollipops or crossed cards: trunks with the right taper, lean, ring scars, fibre and boots; crowns of many arching fronds with individual leaflets that catch the light (alpha-tested cutout cards baked from real modelled leaflet geometry, arranged with natural variation, droop and a few dead fronds hanging); broadleaf trees with a believable branch structure and layered leaf clusters that give a full, irregular silhouette with sky showing through; translucency and sheen on leaves as far as the materials allow; the ground meeting of every plant covered (roots, a mulch ring, grass tufts). And the beach and waterfront objects must look like the real thing in real sizes.

You own: art/blender/assets/vegetation/** and art/blender/assets/leisure/** (asset scripts and your underscore-prefixed shared helpers in each), game/assets/vegetation/**, game/assets/leisure/**, game/src/assets/registry/vegetation.js, and a new registry module game/src/assets/registry/leisure.js (add its one line to registry/index.js as the rules describe). Scratch: /tmp/marazul/vegetation/. Another artist is making street furniture and urban props in art/blender/assets/props/; building artists make facades; do not make those.

## What to ship
Plants are placed by the thousand, so they follow strict levels: lod0 for near (trees 2,000 to 4,000 triangles, palms 1,500 to 3,000, shrubs 300 to 900), lod1 at about a quarter, and lod2 as a baked impostor of two or three crossed cards (under 30 triangles) whose texture you render from the detailed plant, so that a distant avenue of palms still looks right; all levels share the plant's atlas. Give each tree two or three shape variants as nodes inside one file sharing the atlas (variant_a, variant_b...; if the pipeline's level naming needs a per-variant prefix or suffix, choose a clear scheme and document it at the top of the registry), so that a row is not a row of clones. Mark in the extras or the registry which parts sway in the wind (the run-time bends vertices by height and by a mask in vertex colour: paint the crown and frond tips for strong movement and the trunk for none, and say which colour channel you used). One collider per tree: col_cyl_trunk. Leaves are cutout role; trunks and solid parts bake into the atlas.
Vegetation (asset ids): royal_palm (tall, smooth grey trunk with a green crownshaft; refine the example), coconut_palm (curved, leaning trunks, coconuts), fan_palm (stout, with a skirt of dead fans), date_palm (thick diamond-patterned trunk, dense crown), areca_clump (a clump of slender canes for gardens), banyan_fig (a massive spreading shade tree with aerial roots), flame_tree (a wide umbrella crown with red-orange blossom), sea_grape (low, twisted, round leaves, for dunes), live_oak (broad with hanging moss, for the old quarters and wetlands), mangrove (stilt roots standing in water), bald_cypress (buttressed trunk with knees, for the wetlands), street_tree (a tidy medium broadleaf for avenues), shrubs_tropical (one file of several: hibiscus with flowers, bougainvillea mound in magenta, croton, bird-of-paradise, agave, yucca, clipped box hedge segment of 2 m, low round topiary, oleander), ground_cover (one file: grass tufts, tall reeds and cattails for the wetlands, dune grass, ferns, flower-bed patches, lily pads, fallen palm fronds, seaweed wrack). About fourteen files, at most about 300 kB each (a 1024 atlas; palms that fill the cover picture may use 2048 for the base colour if it shows) and 3.2 MiB in all.
Leisure (asset ids; group objects as named nodes in set files sharing an atlas, with a per-object class in the extras or registry: fixed, or knockable with a mass in kilograms): set_beach (parasols open and closed in striped canvas tinted through a paint_canvas role, sun loungers with and without a towel, a lifeguard tower on stilts with a ramp and a flag pole, a volleyball net with posts, surfboards standing in a rack and lying, a beach shower post, a windbreak, a cooler box, beach balls, sandcastle, a rescue board, a pedal boat and a kayak on the sand, a catamaran dinghy with a furled sail), set_waterfront (dock sections of planks on piles in 4 m lengths with a ladder, mooring bollards and cleats, a life-ring post, a fish-cleaning table, lobster traps, coiled rope, a harbour light on a post with lamp_main and anchor_light, a channel marker buoy in red and green with lamp_beacon, a wooden rowing boat, a small moored sailing yacht and a cabin cruiser as static scenery for the marina, pier railing segments of 2 m, a coin telescope, a pier bench), set_park (a playground swing and slide, picnic table, barbecue stand, a fountain basin in stone of about 5 m with a centre piece and anchor_water for the jets, a bandstand or gazebo, a statue plinth with an abstract bronze, park lamp reuse is not needed, a basketball hoop on a post, exercise bars, a dog-waste bin, a wooden footbridge of 6 m for the wetlands, a boardwalk section of 4 m with rails, an airboat as static scenery for the wetlands). Three files, at most about 520 kB each and 1.5 MiB in all; set ASSET['budget'] with the reason.

Build royal_palm and coconut_palm first and judge them against a warm low sun and sky, in Cycles and in the engine viewer, from five metres and from sixty: iterate until both distances convince, then banyan_fig and flame_tree to prove the broadleaf method, then the rest, then the leisure sets. In the registry, list for every file its variants or objects with node names, heights, classes, anchors, colliders, lamp roles and the wind mask; that table is what the city assembler works from. Repeat it in your report.
